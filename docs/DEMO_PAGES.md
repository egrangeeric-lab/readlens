# ReadLens: demo pages for the video

Three original texts written by the team, free to use. Print each on its own A4 / Letter sheet:
- one column, left aligned, no images, no headers or footers;
- 16–18 pt, a plain font (Arial, Calibri or Times), black on white matte paper;
- title in bold, one blank line between paragraphs.

They contain many b / d / p / q letters (to show the highlight) and multi-syllable words (to show syllable colours).
Page C has two paragraphs so the "photographer frame" can capture only the second one (video shot 8).

---

## Page A: The Paper Boat

**The Paper Boat**

Daniel folded a paper boat and placed it in the puddle by the playground. A breeze pushed the boat past a pebble, under a bridge of branches, and round a bend. Daniel jumped and splashed beside it, laughing. "Quick, before it disappears!" shouted his big sister Bella. The boat bobbed and drifted, brave and bright, until it bumped into a duck. The puzzled duck quacked, then paddled away. Daniel picked up his dripping boat and decided it deserved a medal.

## Page B: Dad's Bike

**Dad's Bike**

Every Sunday, Dad and I ride bikes beside the river. My bike is blue, with a bell and a basket. Dad's bike is old and squeaky, but he says it is dependable. Today we pedalled past a bakery, a library and a busy park. We stopped by the pond to watch the dragonflies. Dad bought two peaches and a bread pudding to share. On the way back, it began to drizzle. We didn't mind. We put up our hoods and pedalled home, dripping and happy.

*(85 words, plus title)*

## Page C: The Quiet Planet

**The Quiet Planet**

Far beyond the brightest stars, there is a quiet planet called Bodaq. Its deep blue oceans bubble, and purple plants bend in the breeze.

A curious robot named Pip explores the planet every day. Pip doubles back to study a bridge of pebbles and a peculiar pond that changes colour. Pip records every discovery in a big digital notebook. Probably, one day, people will visit Bodaq. Until then, Pip quietly keeps a diary of the planet, page by page.

*(79 words, plus title)*
