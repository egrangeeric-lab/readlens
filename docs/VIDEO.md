- Real in-headset footage only. No AI-generated video.

## Voice-over script (read as is)

Short sentences, one idea each. Pause at each line break.

> This is a normal page. For a child with dyslexia, it can be a wall.
>
> Letters move. b looks like d. Lines slide into each other.
>
> ReadLens is a mixed-reality reading aid for Quest 3.
>
> Hold up a page. Tap your thumb. The same page comes back, written the way you read best.
>
> The first time, ReadLens doesn't ask hard questions. It shows two versions.
>
> Which one is easier? Tap it. Four taps, and your reading profile is ready.
>
> There are three ways to capture. One: hold the page and read it.
>
> Two: frame just the part you want, like a photographer.
>
> Three: just press Capture.
>
> Your pages stay together, like a book. Slide your thumb to turn them. Your hands can stay on the desk.
>
> Too much text? Focus shows one sentence at a time, big and calm.
>
> Everything can change later: font, spacing, colours. ReadLens warns you when colours get hard to read.
>
> Move the window where you like.
>
> Text is read on the headset. No picture is saved or sent.
>
> ReadLens. Any page, your way.

About 190 words: comfortable at a calm pace in 2:30.

## Filming tips for Quest

1. **Move your head slowly.** Recordings show exactly what the headset sees; fast turns make viewers sick and blur the text. Turn your head about half as fast as feels natural, and hold each key moment still for 2–3 seconds.
2. **Light the page well.** OCR needs even light: a desk lamp or daylight from the side, no strong shadow from your head or hands, no glossy paper glare. If "Page detected" does not appear, add light before moving closer.
3. **Print large.** Print the demo pages at 16–18 pt, black on white matte paper, one text per page. Hold the page about 40 cm away, flat and facing you.
4. **Record each shot separately.** One short clip per shot (start/stop the recorder each time), then cut in an editor. Restart a clip rather than fixing a mistake live. Record voice-over afterwards, in a quiet room.
5. **Check passthrough and the window in a test recording first.** Record 10 seconds, copy it to the computer and check that the real room (passthrough) and the sharp ReadLens window both appear, at the expected resolution. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification pops up, and close the system menu before each take.
